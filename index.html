Phishing is a common method used by cybercriminals to trick people into revealing sensitive information or installing malicious software. Recognizing phishing attempts is an important skill for staying safe online.

Tips to Spot Phishing Emails:

  • Check the sender's email address for misspellings or unusual domains.
  • Look for generic greetings like "Dear User" instead of your name.
  • Be cautious of urgent or threatening language.
  • Hover over links to see the actual URL before clicking.
  • Never enter personal information on a site you accessed via an email link.
  • Don't trust, verify.
